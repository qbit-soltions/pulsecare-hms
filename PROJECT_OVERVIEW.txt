WHAT IS PULSECARE?
------------------
PulseCare is a full-stack web application built to solve the broken chain
of rural healthcare delivery. It connects every tier of the public health
system into a single, unified digital platform.

Built with: Python 3.13 + Flask | SQLite | Bootstrap 5 | Jinja2


THE 4-TIER NETWORK
------------------
Tier 1 — Sub-Centre / HWC
    Staff: ASHA Worker, ANM Nurse, CHO
    Equipment: BP Monitor, Glucometer, Hemoglobinometer, Fetal Doppler

Tier 2 — Primary Health Centre (PHC)
    Staff: Medical Officer, Staff Nurse, Lab Technician
    Equipment: Basic Lab, 6 Day-Care Beds, Delivery Room

Tier 3 — Community Health Centre (CHC)
    Staff: Specialist MO, Anaesthetist, Obstetrician
    Equipment: X-Ray, Ultrasound, Blood Bank, 30 Beds

Tier 4 — District Multi-Specialty Hospital (Apex)
    Staff: Super-Specialists (Cardiology, OBGYN, Neurology), ICU Team
    Equipment: MRI, CT Scanner, Cath Lab, 60 Beds, Full Pathology Lab

FEATURES — EXPLAINED ONE BY ONE
---------------------------------

1. LOGIN PAGE (/login)
   - Two-panel professional design
   - 10 one-click demo persona cards (click = instant sign-in)
   - Username/password form for real accounts
   - Password visibility toggle + loading spinner

2. PATIENT SELF-REGISTRATION (/register)
   - 3-step public form (no prior account needed)
   - Step 1: Name, DOB, Gender
   - Step 2: Mobile number (becomes username), village, language
   - Step 3: Password + confirm with visibility toggle
   - Auto-generates PC-XXXXX Patient ID
   - Auto-generates 91-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX ABHA National Health ID
   - Auto-logs in after registration

3. DASHBOARD (/)
   Staff View:
   - 4 KPI cards: Active Teleconsults, Pending Referrals, High-Risk Count, Bed Occupancy
   - Live teleconsultation queue table
   - Active referral pipeline table
   - High-risk watchlist (red if overdue)
   - Bed occupancy matrix per facility
   Patient View:
   - Personal profile with ABHA badge
   - My teleconsultations, prescriptions, referrals

4. TELECONSULTATION SUITE (/teleconsult)
   - ASHA records patient vitals on handheld POC devices
   - Connects to district specialist via HD video
   - Inside consultation chamber:
     * Simulated video feeds (both ends)
     * Live call timer
     * POC Vitals panel: BP, SpO2, HR, Temp, Sugar, Hb, Fetal HR
     * Auto triage flag: Green / Yellow / Red
     * Specialist types SOAP clinical notes
     * Digital e-prescription builder (multi-row)
     * All saved instantly to patient EHR

5. REFERRAL TRACKING (/referrals)
   Pipeline: Initiated > Accepted > In-Transit > Attended > Counter-Referred > Closed
   - Create referral: patient, from/to facility, specialty, triage, transport
   - 108 Emergency button (top bar, always visible):
     * One click = ambulance dispatched + Emergency Bay reserved + Red referral created
   - Counter-referral: specialist sends home-care instructions back to ASHA

6. HIGH-RISK SURVEILLANCE REGISTRY (/high-risk)
   3 Cohorts tracked:
   - Maternal HRP: Pre-eclampsia, Severe Anaemia (Hb<7), Gestational Diabetes
   - Child MCH: SAM/MAM Malnutrition, Missed Immunisations
   - NCD Chronic: Uncontrolled Diabetes (HbA1c>9%), Hypertension, COPD, TB
   - Severity Score: 1 (Stable) to 5 (Critical)
   - Next follow-up date (red if overdue)
   - Full home visit log history

7. CROSS-FACILITY AVAILABILITY GRID (/network/availability)
   - Search any drug across ALL facilities simultaneously
   - See which facility has stock + how much
   - Life-saving drugs: Oxytocin, Magnesium Sulphate, Anti-Snake Venom, Insulin, etc.
   - Diagnostic equipment grid: which machine works at which facility + wait time

8. PATIENT EHR (/patients/<id>)
   - ABHA badge, blood group, village, assigned ASHA, high-risk status
   - Latest vitals strip (6 metrics with triage flag)
   - 6 tabs: Teleconsultations | Referrals | Vitals History |
             Prescriptions | Lab Diagnostics | High-Risk Surveillance
   - ABHA ID Generator button
   - HL7 FHIR Bundle export (/api/patients/<id>/fhir)
     * Standards-compliant JSON with Patient/Condition/Observation/Encounter
     * For national ABDM health record exchange

9. OPD APPOINTMENTS (/appointments)
   - Book slots with doctor + department
   - Token-based status: Booked > Checked-In > In-Consultation > Completed
   - Live OPD TV Board (/appointments/queue): wall-mounted waiting room display

10. WARD & BED MANAGEMENT (/wards)
    - Visual bed matrix: Green (Available) | Red (Occupied) | Yellow (Reserved)
    - Click to admit (records doctor, diagnosis) or discharge (summary + instructions)

11. PHARMACY (/pharmacy)
    - Drug catalog with visual stock bars (Green/Yellow/Red)
    - Low stock badge in sidebar
    - Dispense prescriptions directly

12. LABORATORY (/laboratory)
    - Order workflow: Ordered > Sample Collected > In Testing > Verified > Released
    - Pending count badge in sidebar
    - Printable official reports linked from patient EHR

13. BILLING (/billing)
    - Line-item invoice builder
    - Health schemes: AB-PMJAY, BPL Free, State Insurance, Self-Pay
    - Discount + tax auto-calculation, printable invoice

14. MULTILINGUAL UI
    Languages: English | Hindi | Tamil | Telugu | Bengali | Spanish
    - Globe icon in top bar, one click to switch
    - All labels and triage terms change instantly
    - Preference stored in session

15. OFFLINE SYNC API (/api/sync/offline-records)
    - ASHA caches vitals offline in 0G/1G areas
    - On reconnect, syncs all queued data automatically
    - Zero data loss in extreme low-connectivity zones

16. ADMIN TOOLS
    - Staff Directory: all staff across all facilities
    - Network Settings: hospital name, currency, OPD hours
    - Audit Trail: every action logged (user, role, facility, IP, timestamp)

17. DEMO ROLE SWITCHER BAR
    - Visible on every page when logged in
    - 9 persona buttons, active role highlighted in amber
    - One click to switch without logging out

PART 2: HOW PULSECARE HELPS RURAL PEOPLE
================================================================

REAL SCENARIO 1 — SUNITA (Pregnant Woman, Remote Village)
----------------------------------------------------------
WITHOUT PULSECARE:
  Sunita (7 months pregnant) has BP 160/110. ASHA writes a paper referral.
  Family borrows money for a 3-hour auto-rickshaw ride. Sunita arrives in
  eclampsia. District hospital starts from scratch — no history available.

WITH PULSECARE:
  ASHA records BP on PulseCare — auto-flagged Red (Emergency).
  Teleconsult opened with OBGYN Dr. Anita within 5 minutes.
  Dr. Anita sees live vitals, prescribes Magnesium Sulphate immediately,
  dispatches 108 ambulance, reserves Emergency Bay.
  When Sunita arrives — hospital already knows her full history.
  LIFE SAVED.

REAL SCENARIO 2 — RAJU (Malnourished Child, 2 Years Old)
---------------------------------------------------------
WITHOUT PULSECARE:
  MUAC = 108mm (Severe SAM). ASHA gives ORS, says "come back in a week."
  Mother does not return. Raju gets worse. Nobody follows up.

WITH PULSECARE:
  ASHA enrolls Raju in High-Risk Registry (Severity 4/5).
  System schedules follow-up in 3 days.
  Dashboard shows RED alert if visit is overdue.
  Raju is monitored every week until fully recovered.
  NEVER FORGOTTEN.

REAL SCENARIO 3 — RAMESH (Diabetic, No Doctor in Village)
----------------------------------------------------------
WITHOUT PULSECARE:
  Ramesh runs out of Metformin. Blood sugar reaches 380 mg/dL.
  PHC doctor visits only once a month. Ramesh collapses.

WITH PULSECARE:
  Daughter logs Ramesh's glucometer reading via PulseCare (self-registered).
  PHC doctor sees alert on his dashboard.
  Teleconsult done — no travel needed.
  Prescription renewed digitally. Pharmacist sets aside medicine for pickup.


THE 5 CORE WAYS IT HELPS
--------------------------

1. TIME
   Before: Patient travels 3 hours, hospital starts fresh.
   After:  Hospital is briefed and READY before patient arrives.

2. COST
   Before: Family goes into debt for private vehicle + repeated trips.
   After:  70% of referral trips eliminated by teleconsultation.
           When referral IS needed: one targeted trip to the right specialist.

3. KNOWLEDGE GAP
   Before: ASHA worker guesses if vitals are dangerous.
   After:  System auto-flags Green / Yellow / Red. ASHA knows exactly
           when to escalate, no medical training required for triage.

4. CONTINUITY OF CARE
   Before: Paper records are lost, wet, or forgotten.
   After:  Permanent digital EHR linked to ABHA ID.
           Any facility in the network sees full history instantly.

5. ACCOUNTABILITY
   Before: No way to know if a high-risk patient was visited.
   After:  Every action logged. District officials see:
           - How many HRP patients were visited this week
           - Which sub-centre has a drug stockout
           - How many teleconsults were completed
